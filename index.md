## 01 — The boat

This is a **full-displacement trawler yacht**. She travels at hull speed rather than
climbing onto a plane, which is why she can carry a modest 250 hp, burn little at
eight knots, and keep her motion predictable when the weather turns.

The hull is **welded steel**, fabricated by a certified and experienced marine welder —
a material chosen for strength, repairability and a working life measured in decades
rather than seasons. The design target is **ISO/CE Category A**, the ocean category: a
boat conceived for extended voyages and self-sufficiency, not fair-weather afternoons.

At **44 feet length over all** she is comfortable in smaller marinas, and with a
**low-profile flybridge** and a **folding radar arch** she is designed to be Great Loop
compatible. Range approaches **1,000 nautical miles** at cruise, so the passage plan
can be about where you want to go rather than where the next fuel dock is.

> “We build pleasure craft, ship-like, not toy-like.”
> — The builder's motto

## 02 — Key features

1. **Full-displacement hull** — a hull that moves through the water instead of over it.
   Lower power, lower fuel burn, and a seakindly motion in a seaway.
2. **Welded steel construction** — all steel fabrication by a certified, experienced
   marine welder. Steel forgives a grounding, can be repaired anywhere, and does not
   fatigue like a lightweight structure.
3. **Long range** — approaching 1,000 nautical miles at cruise, roughly 125 running
   hours at eight knots.
4. **Category A design** — designed to meet ISO/CE Category A, the ocean category.
5. **Right-sized, heavy-duty power** — 250 hp Cummins 6.7 L, warranted for continuous
   duty, fully certified to US EPA Tier 3. Eight knots at a quiet 1,800 rpm.
6. **Service you can actually find** — Cummins marine engines are among the most
   trusted in North America, with service widely available.
7. **Loop-ready profile** — low-profile flybridge, folding radar arch, 44 ft LOA.
8. **A coating system, not a paint job** — white-metal blast, AwlGrip rust-inhibiting
   epoxy, AwlCraft urethane topcoat.

## 04 — Construction and finish

Steel is fabricated by a certified, experienced marine welder. After abrasive blasting
to **white metal** — the preparation a rust-inhibiting epoxy needs — the hull receives
**AwlGrip** rust-inhibiting epoxy coatings, finished with an **AwlCraft** urethane
topcoat for colour, gloss and UV resistance. Blast, prime and finish are treated as one
system, in that order.

## 05 — Great Loop

The Great Loop is the inland circuit through the Intracoastal Waterway, the Great
Lakes, the rivers and Canada. It rewards modest air draft, modest size and long legs
between fuel stops. Four decisions point at that trip:

- **Low-profile flybridge** — keeps air draft within inland bridge clearances.
- **Folding radar arch** — drops the highest point of the boat when a bridge or lock
  demands it, and makes a road or ship move easier.
- **44 ft length over all** — fits smaller marinas, town docks and locks.
- **Range approaching 1,000 nm** — fuel stops become a convenience, not the itinerary.

Great Loop compatibility is a **design intent**. Bridge clearances depend on load,
ballast, fuel and final outfit, and change with water level; ask for current air draft,
draft and clearance figures before planning a route.

to white metal, an AwlGrip rust-inhibiting epoxy system, and an AwlCraft urethane
topcoat.

**Can I see the drawings, and what does it cost?** The current specification, general
arrangement drawings and coating schedule are available to prospective owners. Pricing,
