This trawler yacht is a full displacement boat. It is built of steel and is long range capable.   It is designed to meet ISO/CE category A standards.  It is powered by a 250 hp Cummins 6.7  liter engine.  This heavy duty engine is warrantied for continuous duty and is fully certified for US EPA Tier3 emissions standards. Cummins marine engines are among the most trusted in North America with service widely available.  With this engine, it will have a cruising speed of 8 knots running at a quiet 1800 rpm and is expected to have a range approaching 1000 nautical miles. 

With a low profile fly-bridge and a folding radar arch, it will be Great Loop compatible.  At 44 feet length over-all, it will be comfortable in smaller marinas.

 All steel fabrication is by a certified, experienced marine welder and completed, after sandblasting to white metal, with AwlGip rust inhibiting epoxy coatings . It is finished with an AwlCraft urethane top coat.

Our motto is:  We build pleasure craft, "ship-like, not toy-like".
