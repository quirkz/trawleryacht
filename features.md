## Two paragraphs (≈100 words)

This is a full-displacement trawler yacht in welded steel, 44 feet length over all and
built for long-range cruising rather than weekend speed. A 250 hp Cummins 6.7 L
marine diesel — heavy duty, warranted for continuous duty, US EPA Tier 3 certified —
gives an 8-knot cruise at a quiet 1,800 rpm with range approaching 1,000 nautical
miles.

The design target is ISO/CE Category A, the ocean category. A low-profile flybridge
and folding radar arch keep air draft in check, and 44 feet LOA suits smaller marinas,
so she is designed to be Great Loop compatible. Steel is fabricated by a certified
marine welder and coated after a white-metal blast with AwlGrip epoxy and an AwlCraft
urethane topcoat.

## Wording rules

Use these phrasings; they match the builder's statement and survive scrutiny.

| Say | Not this | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Designed to meet ISO/CE Category A” | “CE Category A certified” | Category A is the design target; certification is a separate process. |
| “Range approaching 1,000 nm” | “1,000-mile range” | The builder states an expectation, not a guarantee. |
| “Full-displacement” / “displacement hull” | “semi-displacement” or “fast trawler” | The hull travels at hull speed; it does not plane. |
| “44 ft length over all (13.4 m)” | “44 m” or “44 ft at the waterline” | LOA is the published dimension; 13.4 m is a conversion. |
| “Great Loop compatible by design” | “Great Loop certified” or “fits every bridge” | Clearances depend on load, ballast and outfit. |
| “Warranted for continuous duty” | “unlimited hours” | Quote the rating, not an interpretation of it. |
| “AwlGrip epoxy system / AwlCraft urethane topcoat” | “AwlGrip paint” | AwlGrip is the epoxy system; AwlCraft is the topcoat. |

## Do not claim

- Beam, draft, air draft, displacement, tankage, accommodation or berth counts — not published.
- Price, delivery date, availability or build slots — not published; enquire.
- Top speed or fuel burn — not published.
- A specific CE certificate, hull number or build location — not published.
- Anything about the boat's speed, range or certification as measured fact — these are
  stated design targets.

## What is genuinely different

1. **Steel, done properly.** The material choice is the strategy: strength and
   repairability, with a coating system built on a white-metal blast.
2. **Displacement economics.** 250 hp moves a 44-foot boat at 8 knots for roughly
   125 hours between fill-ups — the opposite of a planing hull's trade.
3. **Loop-shaped decisions.** Flybridge height, folding arch and 44 ft LOA are choices
   made for the Intracoastal, the locks and the small marinas.
4. **A stated philosophy.** “We build pleasure craft, ship-like, not toy-like.”
